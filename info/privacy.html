Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Word Jam and Word Jam 2 ("we", "our", or "the Games") handle information when you use our mobile applications.

Information We Collect

We are committed to protecting your privacy. We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

We may collect the following anonymous, non-personal information:

  • Device information – device type, operating system version, and screen resolution.
  • Usage data – app launch events, session duration, screens visited, and general gameplay statistics.
  • Advertising identifiers – anonymous device advertising IDs used to serve relevant ads (e.g., Apple's IDFA). You can reset or limit this identifier in your device settings.
  • Crash and performance data – anonymous diagnostic information to help us improve app stability.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics and similar tools to collect anonymous usage statistics. This helps us understand how players interact with the Games so we can improve the experience. Analytics data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify individual users.

Advertising

The Games display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob). These networks may use anonymous identifiers and non-personal data to serve ads, including interest-based advertising. We do not share any personally identifiable information with advertisers.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • iOS – Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track."

Third-Party Services

The Games may integrate the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics / Google AdMob
  • Apple (App Store, Game Center)

Children's Privacy

The Games are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can delete it.

Data Retention

Anonymous analytics data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy. Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to delete.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the Games after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact Us
